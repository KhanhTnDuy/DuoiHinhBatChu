Thu muc anh TAI NGUYEN cua app (hien chi co ma QR ung ho).
(Anh cau hoi KHONG de o day - anh cau hoi nam trong Assets/CauHoi va duoc nap
vao co so du lieu.)

Quy uoc: TEN FILE CHINH LA TEN TAI NGUYEN, duoi file gi cung duoc (png, jpg,
jpeg, webp, bmp, gif). Khoang trang, gach noi va hoa/thuong deu bo qua.

Tai nguyen app dang tim (xem AppImageLocator, LoginWindow.LoadQrImage):

  qr-ung-ho    -> ma QR ung ho nha phat trien.
                  ("QR UNG HO.jpg" hay "qr_ung_ho.png" deu duoc.)
                  Hien trong bang huong dan "?" o man dang nhap.
                  Anh vuong (vd 512x512) se dep nhat.

Khong co anh nay thi man dang nhap hien o gach dut chi cho dat anh, khong loi.
