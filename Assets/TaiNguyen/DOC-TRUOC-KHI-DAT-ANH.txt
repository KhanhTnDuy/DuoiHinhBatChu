cung duoc (png, jpg, jpeg, webp, bmp, gif).

Cac tai nguyen app dang tim:

  qr-ung-ho    -> ma QR ung ho nha phat trien.
                  (dat ten "QR UNG HO.jpg" hay "qr_ung_ho.png" deu duoc:
                  khoang trang, gach noi va hoa/thuong deu bo qua)
                  Hien trong bang huong dan "?" o man dang nhap.
                  Anh vuong (vd 512x512) se dep nhat.

Thieu file nao thi cho do hien o gach dut chi cho dat, app van chay binh thuong.
