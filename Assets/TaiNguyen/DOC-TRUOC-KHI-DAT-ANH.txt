Thu muc anh TAI NGUYEN cua app: ma QR ung ho, logo, hinh trang tri...
(Anh cau hoi KHONG de o day - anh cau hoi nam trong Assets/CauHoi va duoc nap
vao co so du lieu.)

Quy uoc giong het anh cau hoi: TEN FILE CHINH LA TEN TAI NGUYEN, duoi file gi
cung duoc (png, jpg, jpeg, webp, bmp, gif).

Cac tai nguyen app dang tim:

  qr-ung-ho    -> ma QR ung ho nha phat trien.
                  (dat ten "QR UNG HO.jpg" hay "qr_ung_ho.png" deu duoc:
                  khoang trang, gach noi va hoa/thuong deu bo qua)
                  Hien trong bang huong dan "?" o man dang nhap.
                  Anh vuong (vd 512x512) se dep nhat.

  menu-trai    -> anh o COT TRAI cua menu Co dien.
  menu-phai    -> anh o COT PHAI cua menu Co dien.

                  Menu chia ba cot: giua la ba lua chon (Bat dau choi / Choi moi
                  / Bang xep hang), hai ben la hai anh nay.

                  O dat anh: rong 230 px, cao khoang 420 px (cao het phan con
                  lai cua man hinh) -> anh DOC, ti le xap xi 1:1.8.
                  Nen ve 460 x 840 cho net tren man hinh HiDPI.

                  Hai anh nen la mot cap di voi nhau (vd hai nhan vat quay mat
                  vao giua), vi nguoi choi luon nhin thay ca hai cung luc.

Ca hai anh deu duoc cat theo kieu "phu day o" (UniformToFill): anh se bi cat
bot phan thua chu khong bi bop meo. De y tam anh nam giua.

Thieu file nao thi cho do hien o gach dut chi cho dat, app van chay binh thuong.

  dang-nhap-nen     -> anh NEN cot trai man dang nhap (giao dien sang).
  dang-nhap-nen-toi -> ban cho giao dien toi; thieu thi dung lai ban sang.

                  Cot trai rong het phan con lai sau cot form 420 px: tren
                  man 1920x1080 la ~1500 x 1050 -> anh NGANG, ti le xap xi
                  3:2 (nen ve 1920 x 1280 hoac 2400 x 1600 cho net HiDPI).

                  Logo + chu "DUOI HINH / BAT CHU" nam o mep TRAI, giua theo
                  chieu doc, chiem khoang 1/4 chieu rong. App tu phu mot dai
                  mo (mau nen theme) len 1/4 do de chu doc duoc, nen phan
                  trai anh cu de thoang / it chi tiet, dat nhan vat va diem
                  nhan o nua PHAI (nhung dung sat mep phai vi bi cat).

                  Cat kieu phu day o nhu hai anh menu. Thieu file thi cot
                  trai giu nen mo + bau troi nhu cu.
